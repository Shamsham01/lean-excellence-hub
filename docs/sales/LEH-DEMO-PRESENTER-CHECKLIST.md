# Presenter checklist

Use this before a Lean Excellence Hub product demonstration. The booking itself lives in Cal.com. This checklist is for the person running the meeting.

## Customer

- [ ] Open the booking in Cal.com and read the answers: name, business email, organisation, role, approximate number of sites, and the challenge they named.
- [ ] Note their words for the current system and the friction. Do not rewrite them into a slogan.
- [ ] Confirm the attendee list and the meeting link from the Cal.com confirmation or the connected calendar. Do not invent a conferencing link.
- [ ] If they rescheduled or cancelled, use the Cal.com booking. Do not keep a parallel appointment list in LEH.

## Environment

- [ ] Sign in to the approved fictional demo organisation and confirm the home, Gemba, actions, problem solving and projects paths open.
- [ ] Use the demo persona prepared for sales, with only the access that persona should have.
- [ ] Confirm the records you will click are fictional. The local development seed is Apex Manufacturing. CookieWorks is a QA tenant. Harbourfield Foods Group — Bodmin Bakery is the story in the playbook. None of these are a customer organisation.
- [ ] Confirm the screen contains no real customer organisation, no real employee names, and no production data.
- [ ] Do not run `db:seed-demo`, `qa:cookie:reset`, `qa:cookie:hosted-reset`, or any other seed or reset against a hosted organisation. Do not rebuild CookieWorks or Harbourfield to prepare for a meeting.
- [ ] If the fictional packaging-line chain is not already present, use the fallback screenshots and say they are fallback.

## Path to rehearse

- [ ] Gemba observation on a packing line.
- [ ] Owned corrective action.
- [ ] Problem-solving case.
- [ ] Improvement project.
- [ ] Benefit still awaiting validation. Nothing marked verified.
- [ ] Learning: a draft standard or a training expectation that a person would publish.
- [ ] One of: maturity framework, 5S, training or skills, organisation structure, permissions. Skip any that fail a smoke check.
- [ ] LeanAI only if it loads. Say that a person reviews and a person decides, and that LeanAI does not publish frameworks, close actions, grant access or purchase site capacity.

## Room and screen

- [ ] Browser zoom at 100%, one window, notifications and extra tabs closed.
- [ ] Appearance set deliberately. Light is the safer screenshare unless the customer asked for dark.
- [ ] Bookmark bar and unrelated accounts hidden.
- [ ] Fallback screenshots available offline if the demo environment fails.
- [ ] The playbook is [LEH-DEMO-PRESENTATION-PLAYBOOK.md](./LEH-DEMO-PRESENTATION-PLAYBOOK.md).

## After the meeting

- [ ] Write the agreed next step, the owner and the timing.
- [ ] Draft the follow-up from [LEH-DEMO-FOLLOW-UP-EMAIL.md](./LEH-DEMO-FOLLOW-UP-EMAIL.md). Send it yourself. LEH does not send it.
- [ ] Do not add savings, logos or quotes that were not said in the meeting.
